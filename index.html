LifeOne 365 Privacy First

Privacy Policy · Last updated: November 15, 2026

LifeOne 365 (app.lifeone365.journal) is a minimalist annual journal and year-progress visualizer for Android. Your privacy and personal memories are of utmost importance. This Privacy Policy describes how the app handles your information.

Summary: LifeOne 365 is 100% offline. We do not require accounts, we do not track you, and we do not operate remote servers to store or view your journal entries or photos.

1. 100% Offline & Private by Design

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, provide an email address, or log in to use the app.
  • No Analytics or Trackers: The app does not include any third-party telemetry, behavioral analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking cookies.
  • No Cloud Backend: We do not operate remote databases or cloud servers to store your memories, notes, photos, or locations.

2. Data Collection and Usage

All data processed by LifeOne 365 remains strictly on your device:

A. Notes, Photos & Stickers

Text, stickers, and photos added to your day canvas are stored in the app's private internal storage directory (context.filesDir).

Photo picking is conducted solely via Android’s modern System Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia). The app does not request broad storage permissions (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) and only accesses images you explicitly select.

B. Approximate Location (Optional)

  • Permission: android.permission.ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
  • Usage: Only when you explicitly tap the "Add Location" tool on a day canvas. The app fetches the current approximate city/locality name to create a location card.
  • Storage: Coordinates are saved strictly into your device's local database. Background location tracking is never used.
  • Google Static Maps: If an optional Google Static Maps API key is configured, coordinate values are transmitted over HTTPS to Google Static Maps solely to render the preview snapshot.

C. Background Tasks & Widgets

  • Permission: android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
  • Usage: Used by Android WorkManager and Broadcast Receivers to re-schedule midnight widget updates after a device reboot or timezone change. No user data is transferred.

3. Data Storage & Manual Backups

Your data is stored locally in an encrypted/sandboxed SQLite database via Android Room.

LifeOne 365 provides an explicit Export / Import Backup feature. When triggered, a .zip file containing your journal database and photos is saved to or restored from a folder chosen by you using Android's Storage Access Framework. You retain complete ownership and control over your backup files.

4. Children’s Privacy

LifeOne 365 does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
